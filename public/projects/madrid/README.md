# Madrid delivery sim recordings

Existing browser recordings from alejandrofrank/madrid-delivery-sim at revision
0f7568dc05383dc348bf97f893c7e13982080cfb, transcoded from the committed GIFs to
H.264 MP4 with original timing, no added frames or audio. Source/output hashes
and sizes are in recordings.json. Posters are the first decoded video frames.

- city-demo.mp4: docs/media/product-flow.gif (8.2 seconds).
- store-demo.mp4: docs/media/store-journey.gif (14 seconds).

Synthetic recorded runs, not a live or hosted simulation. The city recording is
the earlier product-story capture; it is not a new capture of the latest UI.
