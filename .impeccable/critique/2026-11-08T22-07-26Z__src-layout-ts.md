## Cognitive load and visitor journeys

A first-time visitor understands the four homepage destinations, but may miss the opened content below the calendar and must open project names to learn their purpose. A hiring manager cannot scan dates and outcomes across roles through List view. A keyboard visitor gets good native Arcade dialog behavior but weaker containment on the timeline. A returning reader loses Tech's list layout after opening a case. The strongest moment is reaching the actual evidence; shorten the route there.
